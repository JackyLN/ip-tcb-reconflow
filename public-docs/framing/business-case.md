# Business case

ASSUMED USD planning inputs are editable at /reports/impact. They are not Techcombank data.
Monthly capacity = payout volume × manual minutes / 60 × loaded hourly cost × adoption ×
automated proportion. Annual capacity = monthly capacity × 12. Cost = implementation +
12 × monthly operating cost. Net = annual capacity - cost. Low/high capacity is 50%/150% of base;
cost stays fixed. Break-even = total first-year cost / constant monthly capacity value.

The base UI inputs are 10,000 payouts/month, 12 minutes, $15/hour, 60% adoption, 50% automation,
$10,000 implementation, $300/month operation. They are illustrative assumptions. Include AI,
support and training in operating cost. There is no observed staffing reduction or revenue lift.

NOT IMPLEMENTED: month-by-month ramp, separate rework/training assumptions, discounting,
probabilistic sensitivity or realized financial accounting. Measure real baseline case time,
quality and adoption before using this projection for a decision.
