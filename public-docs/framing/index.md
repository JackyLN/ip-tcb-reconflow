# Problem framing

**Hypothesis:** merchant settlement operations need to reconcile provider payout breakdowns with
bank credits. Differences in fees, refunds, timing and references may consume investigation effort
and leave unresolved-case ownership unclear.

This problem has not been validated with Techcombank stakeholders. The prototype demonstrates a
possible workflow and its tradeoffs; it does not claim knowledge of internal bank systems.

## Evidence and decisions

- The [decision memo](decision-memo.md) compares build, buy and do nothing.
- The [discovery record](discovery.md) identifies the real interviews still outstanding.
- The [business case](business-case.md) explains the editable, assumption-based projection.

## Intended result

A supervised workflow should make source evidence, deterministic matching, exception ownership
and independent review inspectable. Any claim of real productivity or financial benefit requires
a measured baseline and pilot; see the [90-day proposal](../rollout/plan.md).
