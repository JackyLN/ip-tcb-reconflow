## Why the failures happened

These are **AI-assisted qualitative observations checked against fixture facts**, not blinded
human semantic grading or proof of the model's internal reasoning. They explain observable
contradictions; the complete failed-case inventory follows.

### Fee policy and net arithmetic were ignored

All six FEE_MISMATCH and six PAYOUT_MISMATCH fixtures failed. Matching declared net to a bank
credit was often treated as sufficient, without checking independent fee and gross-to-net math.
Case 100004 has gross 100,400: 200 bps gives fee 2,008, but observed fee is 2,108. The model
returned MATCHED. For case 100073, 100,300 − 2,006 = 98,294, but declared net is 99,294.
The model nevertheless said fee and net math were consistent.

This benchmark withholds rule findings and computed policy math. The operational application
already supplies deterministic findings/calculated evidence to its advisory investigator. This
run measures raw-evidence diagnosis, not controlled human productivity or overall app matching.
It reinforces the deterministic-finance boundary rather than justifying model-written finance.

### One-day booking was incorrectly called late

Twelve non-late fixtures were labeled LATE_CREDIT even though their one-day booking gap fits the
explicit three-day window. Case 100003 acknowledges that window yet chooses LATE_CREDIT.
Case 100063 invents a same-day timing implication absent from the supplied policy. Self-contradictory
explanations can cite real evidence IDs.

### Duplicate credits were treated as an apparent match

All six AMBIGUOUS cases failed. Case 100033 returned MATCHED with two indistinguishable eligible
credits. It hypothesized repeated extraction and requested distinguishing evidence, but treated
the match as settled before that hypothesis was confirmed. The reference preserves ambiguity.

### Missing gross did not produce abstention

All six INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE cases failed: three MATCHED and three LATE_CREDIT predictions.
Gross was absent, so fee/payout arithmetic could not be verified. Matching available net and
credit does not establish missing arithmetic; inventing a timing rule does not fill that gap.

### Correct observed facts did not guarantee the right label

Five of six AMOUNT_MISMATCH cases failed. Case 100023 correctly states that the fee equals the
200-bps policy and the credit exceeds net by 500, but chooses FEE_MISMATCH. This is a label/evidence
consistency failure rather than inability to read the supplied numbers.

### A citation-format defect was correctly rejected

Case 100043 selected the correct raw LATE_CREDIT label, but cited `evidence_id:payout:100043`
instead of the observed `payout:100043`, with the same extra prefix on the other references.
The server rejected the entire report; it remains incorrect in the accepted-answer score.
References were not silently repaired and the case was not retried. This was not a citation of
the injected `evidence:SECRET` instruction. No arbitrary tool, approval or finance write was executed.

## Limits and next development work

This small templated set shares scenario patterns between 24 development and 48 holdout cases.
It has no clean MATCHED examples; refunds are zero throughout and mixed-error diversity is absent. This holdout is now
observed: do not tune repeatedly on it and call subsequent scores unseen. The deterministic
baseline is fixture-specific. Real-bank accuracy, calibrated confidence, semantic hallucination
rate, high-risk recall and human-control benefit remain NOT MEASURED.

**PROPOSED, NOT EXECUTED:** improve the classification contract using development cases; supply
deterministic arithmetic/window/uniqueness findings as read-only features, matching the product
boundary; check claim/label consistency; require abstention for missing necessary facts; create
a fresh broader holdout before comparing prompt/model/reasoning options. Schema/citation checks
alone are insufficient. No extra paid evaluation or model change followed this run. Independent
domain-expert semantic review is still required for unsupported-claim or safety-rate measurement.
