PROPOSED 90-day rollout; no pilot has been conducted.
Team of five: Product/BA, backend/data, AI/ML, frontend/UX, platform/security/QA.
Days 1–30: interview operations/risk/IT users, validate workflow ownership, collect baseline,
approve data access and retention, build connectors in shadow mode; operations owns labels.
Gate: trustworthy source totals, reconciled replay, risk acceptance and complete audit.
Days 31–60: one-team supervised pilot with staged/control allocation; platform monitors
incidents, AI lead grades evidence and abstention, UX runs training and usability sessions.
Gate (proposed): >=95% eligible batches matched or owned within 24h, no unauthorized writes,
>=99% evidence validity and >=20% shorter investigation time versus observed control.
Days 61–90: expand one provider/team at a time only if gates hold; publish runbooks, owners,
training, rollback and weekly cost/quality review. BA coordinates operations adoption.
Stop: unauthorized action, material arithmetic error, materially unsupported AI claim,
or negative risk-adjusted capacity value. Disable new AI jobs and return to human workflow.
Dependencies: bank sponsor, security/data access approval, real operational ground truth.
Risks: poor source references, adoption lag, model drift, policy variance; retain human review.
